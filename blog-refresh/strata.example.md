# Strata — the eras in this archive

Copy to `strata.md` and fill in from your own corpus. Most archives older than a few years have
two or three, and they usually map to reorganisations: a founding era, a rebrand, an acquisition
whose content was migrated wholesale.

You find them by reading, not by guessing. Sort the corpus by date, read five posts from each
year, and write down what changed.

**How to recognise it**

- [e.g. bolded abstract opener before the first heading]
- [e.g. emoji callouts]
- [e.g. a hard-sell button every few sections]

**On refresh:** **Remediation, not emulation.** If a whole sub-corpus is machine-translated and
off-brand, the right move is to fix or retire those pages — not to rewrite each one lovingly in
the house voice. Decide that once, at the stratum level, rather than page by page.
