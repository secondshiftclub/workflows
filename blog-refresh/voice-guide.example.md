# Voice guide — [your brand]

Copy to `voice-guide.md`. This is the **judgment layer**: the half of your voice a regex can
never check. The mechanical half lives in `config.json` and `voice-profile.json`, enforced by
`scripts/style_lint.py`.

Fill it in from `voice_profile.py` output plus a read of 20–30 of your own best posts. Every
section below should end up with **real examples pulled from your archive**, not descriptions.
A guide without exemplars is a vibe, and nobody can write or review against a vibe.
