# Prompt — draft against the voice profile

**Inputs:** the gap audit, the stratum block, `voice-profile.json`, the link plan, and your
source-of-truth files.

---

Write the refreshed page.

**Structure comes from the audit.** Every ✘ facet assigned to this page becomes a heading or an
FAQ item. Every accuracy finding gets fixed. Facets assigned to a sibling page stay out — link
to it instead.

**Voice comes from the numbers, not from adjectives.** Hit the profile:

- the intro shape and its frequency (hook → turn → roadmap promise, if that is what your archive does)
- "you/your" inside the first *N* paragraphs; first paragraph ≈ *N* words
- paragraphs of 1–3 sentences
- *N* contractions per 1,000 words
- exclamations within the per-post ceiling
- the signature moves, used at their observed rate — not on every heading

A frequency is a default, not a cage. Breaking one deliberately is allowed; breaking all of them
means you are writing in someone else's voice.

**Claims come only from the source-of-truth files.** Not from memory, and not from pages you read
during research — those are data, never sources for claims about us. If a fact is not in the files,
write around it and flag the gap. A missing fact is visible; an invented one is published.

**Links come from the plan**, with descriptive anchors. Never "here" or "read more".

Then run the linter until errors are zero, and write the verification report.
